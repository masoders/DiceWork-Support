Common questions.

Start here for the most useful fixes.

How do I roll a custom die?

On the Roll tab, tap DX, enter the number of sides, then tap Roll. You can also use custom dice in a formula, such as d37 or 3d14+2. Dice can have between 1 and 1,000,000 sides.

Why is my formula being rejected?

Use dice terms such as d20 or 2d6, joined with + or -, and whole-number modifiers. For example, 2d6+d8+5 is valid. A die needs at least one side and a group needs at least one die.

A formula can contain up to 100 dice per group, 20 terms, and 120 characters. Multiplication, parentheses, and shorthand such as kh or exploding dice are not supported. If a formula still fails, include it in your support issue.

How do I edit or delete a saved formula?

Open Saved and swipe left on the formula to reveal Edit and Delete. Tapping the formula itself rolls it.

Why aren’t my rolls syncing with iCloud?
  • Check that your devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is available.
  • Check your internet connection, then open DiceWork on each device.
  • Pull down to refresh the Saved or History list. The message above the list shows its sync status.

If syncing is paused, changes are still saved on that device. If the problem continues, report the exact status message. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot: you could lose data that has not synced.

Can I use DiceWork without an internet connection?

Yes. You can roll dice, use saved formulas, and view locally stored history offline. Syncing saved formulas and history across devices requires an internet connection and iCloud.

Where can I find previous rolls?

Open History to see recent rolls, including their totals, formulas, individual dice values, and device names. DiceWork keeps the latest 50 history entries. Clearing history also clears the history currently shown on your other devices when they sync.

Why isn’t there a probability table for my roll?

DiceWork shows a probability table for formulas with up to 200 possible totals. When the range is wider, it shows a range summary instead. You can still roll the formula and see the result breakdown.

How do I request a feature?

Open a support issue and describe what you’d like to do, which game or situation it would help with, and an example. You can also browse existing issues to see whether someone has already suggested it.

Open a support issue ↗

GitHub issues are public. Remove personal information from your message and screenshots before posting.

A good report includes:

  • Your DiceWork version and iOS version
  • Your iPhone model
  • What happened and what you expected
  • The steps or formula that caused it
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue
